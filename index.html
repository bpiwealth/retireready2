Use of the RetireReady Calculator does not create any attorney-client, accountant-client, fiduciary, or other professional relationship between you and BPI Wealth, or any of its affiliates.

The RetireReady Calculator is based on general provisions of Philippine law, including the National Internal Revenue Code and Civil Code rules on succession. However, laws, regulations, BIR issuances, and jurisprudence may change at any time, and the RetireReady Calculator may not reflect the most current developments.

The estate tax computations generated by the RetireReady Calculator are based on simplified assumptions. For married individuals, all assets are assumed to be conjugal or community property, with one-half (½) attributed to the decedent. The computation includes only the standard deduction and family home deduction, and does not take into account other allowable deductions or specific circumstances that may affect the actual estate tax due.

Accordingly, all results are estimates only and are indicative in nature. They do not substitute for the advice, review, or certification of a licensed CPA, tax practitioner, or legal professional, and should not be relied upon for tax filing, estate planning, or compliance purposes.

1 “Legally Married” refers to your current marital status as of today. You are considered legally married if you are in a valid marriage that has not been annulled or declared void by a competent court, or dissolved by the death of your spouse. If you are only legally separated from your spouse, you remain legally married.

2 “Family Home” refers to the house and/or land on which it stands where the family actually and permanently resides. It is the principal residence of the family, to which they return after temporary absences, such as travel, overseas work, or study.

3 Estimated net return p.a. of retirement fund is a free field. The 10-year average yield of the 10-year Philippine Government Security is 4.25% p.a.

4 Annual Inflation — the average 20-year Philippine inflation rate is 3.75% p.a.

5 Property appreciation p.a. defaults to 5% p.a. Real estate values in the Estate Planning tab are grown at this rate from today to your retirement age, and onward through the post-retirement projection. Actual appreciation varies widely by location and property type.
